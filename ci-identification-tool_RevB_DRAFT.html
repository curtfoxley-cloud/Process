Determine whether your item should be designated as a Configuration Item (CI) per defense standard EIA-649-1A.

Definition: Configuration Item (CI) · §2.3

Any hardware, software, firmware or aggregation that satisfies an end use function and is designated by the Acquirer for separate configuration control. That control includes establishing the CI's requirements and verifying that the CI's configuration meets the requirements and that its configuration documentation accurately describes its configuration.

Reference: EIA-649-1A, §3.2.1 Configuration Item Selection. The Supplier shall select and submit potential CIs for Acquirer review and selection concurrence. Final CI designation authority rests with the Acquirer. This tool is a decision aid only and does not substitute for engineering judgment or contractual requirements.
