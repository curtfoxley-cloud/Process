EIA-649-1A §3.2.1(2)

aThe item is critical to the functioning of the system.
bFailure of the item might result in serious injury, loss of life, or loss of primary system capability.
cThe item’s technology is complex, not well known, and critical to meeting acquisition, operational, or supportability goals of the program.
dThe item is under control as designated by the Acquirer.
eThe item requires separate type designation, versions, or nomenclatures.
fThe item is critical or high risk and failure would have significant financial impact.
gThe item requires one or more logistics elements (e.g., APL/AEL/ACL provisioning lists, test equipment, technical manuals, PMS MIP/MRC, danger/caution tag-out).

Configuration Item. Any hardware, software, firmware, or aggregation thereof that satisfies a system end-use function and is designated for separate configuration management.

CI identifier and part number. The CI identifier follows the end-use function and the need to manage it separately. The part or identifying number follows form, fit, and function, and changes on a non-interchangeable condition. One CI can carry many part numbers.

CI selection is a CM decision. The concurrences confirm the technical basis is sound. They do not make the selection.
