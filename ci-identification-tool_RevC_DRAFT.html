Is the candidate an item, rather than a function, task, failure mode, feature, or location? Only an item can be a CI. A label is a signal that items worth screening exist nearby.
Does the item satisfy a system end-use function? A small part carrying an external criticality designation does satisfy this gate. Failing it here skips the safety screen.
Is the item inside a larger item procured as a unit from the supplier that designed it? If yes, the CI is the item as procured. Screen that instead.

EIA-649-1A §3.2.1(2)

aThe item is critical to the functioning of the system.
bFailure of the item might result in serious injury, loss of life, or loss of primary system capability.
cThe item’s technology is complex, not well known, and critical to meeting acquisition, operational, or supportability goals of the program.
dThe item is under control as designated by the Acquirer.
eThe item requires separate type designation, versions, or nomenclatures.
fThe item is critical or high risk and failure would have significant financial impact.
gThe item requires one or more logistics elements (e.g., APL/AEL/ACL provisioning lists, test equipment, technical manuals, PMS MIP/MRC, danger/caution tag-out).
SWComputer software is designated as a CI (CSCI). A rule of the process, not one of the criteria above.

Safety linkage. Any item identified as safety-critical under the SMS (SM-0001) is screened against criterion (b) and, where applicable, criterion (a). Such items will nearly always qualify. If a safety-critical item is not designated a CI, the rationale requires System Safety concurrence.

Supplemental criteria, GEIA-HB-649A App. D. Independent end-use function; separate change effectivity address; common to several systems; interfaces with an existing CI; interchangeability level; separate delivery or installation; separate performance and test requirements; independently testable; configurable item requiring an accurate record of its configured state; requires change request approval to modify; new or modified design; critical to top-down identification of lower-tier CIs.

Glossary. SMS is the safety management system. SM-0001 is the safety management system standard. APL, AEL and ACL are provisioning lists. PMS MIP/MRC is Planned Maintenance System documentation.

CI selection is a CM decision. The concurrences confirm the technical basis is sound. They do not make the selection. Final designation authority for Acquirer-controlled CIs rests with the Acquirer.
